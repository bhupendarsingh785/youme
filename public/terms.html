Ye ek shuruaati template hai. Public karne se pehle kisi vakil se dikhwa lo aur [apna email], [apna sheher/rajya] bhar do.

Sabke liye seedhe niyam

  • Kisi ko pareshan, dhamki ya gaali mat do. Nafrat mat phailao.
  • Nagnata, yaun shoshan, bachchon se jude galat content bilkul mana hai.
  • Hinsa ya kanoon todne ke liye ukasana mana hai.
  • Spam, dhokha ya kisi aur ban kar jhoot mat bolo.
  • Doosron ka nijee data (number, pata, photo) unki marzi ke bina mat daalo.
  • App ko tod-phod ya zyada load daal kar bigadne ki koshish mat karo.

Report aur block

Kisi post par "⋯" dabao → Report ya Block. 3 alag logon ke report ke baad post sabke liye apne aap chhup jaata hai, jab tak koi use dekh na le. Hum niyam todne wale post hata sakte hain aur account band kar sakte hain.

Tumhara content

Jo tum likhte ya daalte ho wo tumhara hai. Use dikhane ke liye (feed me, doosron ko) tum hume ijazat dete ho. Tum jab chaho post ya poora account delete kar sakte ho.

Coins

Coins abhi sirf app ke andar ke inaam hain. Unki koi paise me keemat nahi hai aur unhe nikala ya becha nahi ja sakta. Spam se coins kamane par hum coins ya account hata sakte hain.

Seva jaisi hai waisi

App "jaisi hai waisi" di jaati hai. Hum poori koshish karte hain ki sab theek chale, par bina rukawat ya galti ke chalne ki guarantee nahi de sakte. Seva kabhi bhi badal ya band ho sakti hai.

Badlav
